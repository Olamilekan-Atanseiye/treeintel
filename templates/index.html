    About

    A field tool built for tropical forestry

    What it does

    Classifies tropical African tree species from a single photograph using a convolutional neural network trained on field-collected specimen imagery.

    Who it's for

    Researchers, field technicians, and students working on forest inventory, biodiversity monitoring, and species conservation across the region.

    What's next

    Carbon stock estimation and an LLM-powered knowledge layer are in development and will connect directly into the dashboard shown above.
